# Task 9 Production decision

For a modest deployment, retain validated measurements in SQLite with `stations`, `readings`, `complaints`, `sentiment_results`, and `daily_summaries` tables. Use foreign keys, uniqueness, numeric checks and an index on `(station_id, observed_at)`. Normalize known Fahrenheit input to Celsius before range validation; reject impossible values with an audit reason and never convert missing readings to zero. Keep source files and line-level rejection logs for traceability.

At 100 additional stations reporting every 15 minutes, the new stream adds 100 x 96 = 9,600 readings per day, or about 3.5 million per year. A single SQLite writer can handle an educational prototype with batched transactions, WAL mode, retention/archival and daily aggregate tables. For multiple concurrent ingest workers, replicas, high availability or production service-level requirements, migrate to PostgreSQL or another managed relational service. The benchmark's 876,000-row scale offers a repeatable local comparison; its actual times and memory are in `../Task08_Loops_vs_Libraries_SQL/benchmark_results/benchmark_results.csv`.

Use CSV for supplier interchange, then validate in a streaming pipeline and batch insert with bound parameters. Use SQLite GROUP BY for scheduled daily summaries and Pandas for exploratory analysis. A word-list scorer is explainable and cheap as triage but misses context, sarcasm, domain vocabulary and multiword scope. The measured 50-complaint review supports limited demonstration only. At 50,000 complaints monthly, store raw complaint text with access controls and retention rules, retain model/version and confidence, and evaluate a trained classifier against a manually labelled set before deployment. Human review remains necessary for complaints that affect enforcement or safety.

Extractive sentence ranking requires tokenizing and scoring words (O(W) time and O(V) frequency-map space for W words and V distinct terms); selecting a first sentence by split is O(W) time in the usual implementation and O(1) retained sentence state, not strictly O(1) input time because text must be scanned. Ranking is more informative when an important concern appears beyond the first sentence, but remains a heuristic rather than generated paraphrase.
